CN SkatePark is a small business started as a dedicated skatepark for young and aspiring skaters and people in need of safe skate parks instead of skating on the streets where you could get run over. It was started by entrepreneur Corlett Nonge in mid 2026 seeing as skaters in South Africa didn’t have many dedicated skate parks.

The business has a clear mission and the vision to accomplish it, that mission being creating a safe space for people to get together and partake in rollerskating, skateboarding, trick biking and many more sports. It envisions spreading branches throughout the country bit by bit becoming the national standard of safe skating.
